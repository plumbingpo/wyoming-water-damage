When to Call for Emergency Water Damage Restoration in Wyoming

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • A leak upstairs is sending water down the walls of the level beneath it.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • A ceiling is sagging or bulging with trapped water above it.
  • A growing pool on the floor is heading for the next room or running downstairs.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • An outlet, a light fixture or the ceiling above has water dripping out of it.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Store valuables and important papers off the floor, especially in basements and lower levels.
  • Test your sump pump before the rainy season and consider a battery backup.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
