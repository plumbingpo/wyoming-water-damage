Moisture Monitoring

Finding and Preventing Water Risks at Your Property

Owning commercial property comes with a duty of care, which means keeping the premises safe. Hidden moisture, aging plumbing, and inadequate waterproofing can result in sudden failures that cause injuries, property damage, and costly lawsuits. Using thermal imaging and moisture monitoring equipment, Wyoming Restoration performs professional risk assessments that check how vulnerable each area is to water getting in. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

These assessments serve a dual purpose: they protect people and property by identifying vulnerabilities, and they demonstrate due diligence that can protect you legally if an incident occurs. Owners who keep up regular professional inspections and follow the experts' advice are viewed favorably by insurers and courts. Every commercial property should have an annual moisture assessment, and high-risk areas, including aging plumbing systems, basements, and mechanical rooms, should be inspected more often.
