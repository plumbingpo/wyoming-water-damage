Common Water Damage Issues in Kalamazoo

Six kinds of calls make up the bulk of our work for Kalamazoo households. They are all common, and with every one of them waiting adds to the repair.

Mold: Most mold growth is the follow-up to an earlier leak that seemed to be taken care of. Surfaces dry with towels and a box fan, yet the pad, subfloor and inside of the wall hold moisture. Since colonies can form in 24 to 48 hours, the first hint is often an earthy smell or speckled drywall weeks later.

Hoses: The braided or rubber lines behind toilets, sinks and washing machines are under pressure all day. A burst connector keeps spraying until somebody closes a valve, and that may take a long while in an empty house. Replacing old connectors and knowing where your shutoff sits are the cheapest protection available.

Pumps: In homes that have one, a sump pump works unnoticed for years and then quits on the one night it is needed. Stuck float switches, tripped breakers and burned-out motors are what we find most. That leaves the lowest level under a few inches of water, with flooring, wall bases and stored belongings soaked. Testing the pump now and then, and adding a battery backup, lowers the odds considerably.

Toilets: An overflowing toilet is the most common bathroom emergency we handle in Kalamazoo. It matters where the water came from, because tank or supply water is clean and used bowl water is not. Either way it moves under tile edges and vanity cabinets, so the floor needs to be checked with a meter.

Cooling: Your air conditioner drains condensation through a line that most owners never see. A rusted pan or a plugged line lets that water run into nearby flooring or the ceiling below. Because it happens slowly, the damage in many Kalamazoo homes is well along before a stain appears. Most of these are avoided by getting the drain cleaned annually ahead of heavy use.

Flooding: When storm drains cannot keep up with a downpour, surface water heads for the lowest doorway it can find. It reaches ground-level rooms, garages and walkout lower levels on the downhill side before anything else. Our Wyoming Restoration crews extract it, remove what cannot be cleaned and dry the structure to verified readings.

What to Do Before Our Kalamazoo Crew Arrives

What you do before help reaches your Kalamazoo address has a real effect on how the job goes. Safety comes before saving anything. If water is near outlets, cords or the electrical panel, stay out of it, and switch off power to that area only if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water. If the water is from a sewer or toilet backup, do not touch it at all.

Then shut the water off. Close the valve at the fixture if there is one, or the main shutoff for the house. After that, carry light items such as rugs, documents, electronics and chairs somewhere dry. Put foil or plastic under the legs of heavy furniture so the wood does not stain the carpet.

Before mopping, record the damage on your phone, since an insurance company will ask what it looked like at the start. If the water is clean and shallow, blot what you can, and let our Kalamazoo crew handle everything else.
