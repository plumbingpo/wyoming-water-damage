Restoration Challenges Flint Homeowners Face

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. The five below are ones we see constantly in Flint, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: A pipe that has split is the most urgent call there is, because it puts out water for as long as the supply is on. Depending on the season, the break may follow a freeze, and it often shows only when the ice inside melts. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. Ceilings that bulge with trapped water should be left alone and the room below kept empty.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. You cannot stop the rain, but you can move belongings up and keep away from any outlets near the water. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. As soon as it is safe to enter, we pump the water out and take away porous items it ruined.

Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. In Flint kitchens we often dry cabinets in place by sending air through small holes in the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Puddles on the ground cover, dripping ductwork or wet insulation between the joists are what an inspection usually turns up. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. We extract the water, dry the wood to a safe reading and tell you what we think the source is.

Odors: A musty smell near the site of an old leak is the least urgent item here, yet it deserves an inspection soon. Since growth can start 24 to 48 hours after materials get wet, an old leak that was never fully dried is a likely culprit. Do not scrub or spray a large patch yourself, because disturbing it spreads spores through the house. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Flint.
