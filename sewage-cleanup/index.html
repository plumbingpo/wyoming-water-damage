Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. When it happens in Wyoming, Wyoming Restoration is available 24/7 with technicians trained for this work, proper protective gear and disinfectants registered with the EPA, everything needed for a safe decontamination.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. Because of it, cleanup follows the toughest rules in the trade, which means full protective gear, barriers to contain the area, tearing out any porous material that met sewage and disinfecting all that is left. For Category 3 water damage the reference is IICRC S500, and our crews follow it to bring the building back to safe occupancy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Residual moisture is taken out with commercial-grade dryers. An industrial deodorizing treatment gets rid of the sewage smell. Finally, a clearance test verifies that people can safely use the area again.

Maintenance Tips for Sewage Cleanup

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Find your sewer cleanout now and check its condition, so it can be reached in an emergency
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Fit a strainer over each sink and shower drain so hair and debris stay out of the pipes

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. Exposure to sewage pathogens can cause serious illness, including gastrointestinal disease, hepatitis, respiratory infections and skin infections.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in Wyoming

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Soggy, smelly ground over the sewer line or leakage at the cleanout cap
  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Sewage residue visible around drain openings or on basement floors
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Dark, dirty water or sewage rising out of a tub, toilet or floor drain
