Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Take dated photos of the house every year, since insurers ask about prior condition
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Learn where the main water shutoff is and how to close it if pipes are damaged in a storm
  • Make sure the ground around the house slopes away and downspouts discharge well clear of it
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should

Signs You Need Storm Damage Repair in Wyoming

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Puddles near skylights, beside a chimney or on the attic floor
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Water in the lowest level after storm drains back up or overflow

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's what you can expect when you choose Wyoming Restoration:
